# Marketing-Attribution-Campaign-Performance-Dashboard

An interactive **Marketing Attribution & Campaign Performance Dashboard** designed to evaluate marketing spend, revenue generation, campaign effectiveness, customer conversions, and regional performance.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of marketing KPIs and helps identify the channels and campaigns delivering the strongest returns.

##  Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze marketing campaign performance and understand how different **channels, campaigns, regions, objectives, and customer segments** contribute to revenue and conversions.

The dashboard enables marketers and business teams to make **data-driven decisions about marketing budgets, campaign optimization, and customer acquisition**.
## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **DAX** – KPI and marketing metric calculations
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and source data

##  Marketing Metrics

### ROAS

**Return on Ad Spend** measures revenue generated for every unit of marketing spend.

**Formula:**

`ROAS = Total Revenue / Total Spend`

A higher ROAS generally indicates more efficient marketing spending.

### Conversion Rate

**Conversion Rate** measures the percentage of leads that result in conversions.

**Formula:**

`Conversion Rate = Total Conversions / Total Leads × 100`

### Revenue Efficiency

Revenue can be compared against marketing spend to identify channels that provide stronger financial returns.
